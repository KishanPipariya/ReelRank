## Cognitive load

Moderate: 2 of 8 checklist failures. Related controls are grouped and advanced options use progressive disclosure. The filter groups still span several categories, and the two top-level disclosures—“Filters” and “Catalogue tools”—may make control locations hard to learn. The sort menu has four enabled choices plus a disabled “Relevance” option; country, language, decade, genre, and source-list indexes can also contain many choices, with search helping to narrow them.

## What's working

- The visual language and product idea reinforce each other; this reads as a personal film catalogue rather than a generic list.
- List counts, score details, and source-list links help users inspect why films rank where they do.
- Source includes responsive layout rules, keyboard shortcuts, loading and stale-state messaging, and visible focus styling.

## Priority issues

1. **[P1] Catalogue controls may be hard to scan.** Search and watched filters sit beside separate “Filters” and “Catalogue tools” disclosures, each with nested sections. New users may not know where to find sorting, saved views, or a particular facet. **Fix:** clarify the distinction between discovery filters and catalogue actions, and make common actions easier to locate. **Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify`.
2. **[P2] Advanced filtering has substantial choice depth.** People and lists, place and language, runtime, genres, and decades create a broad decision space. **Fix:** prioritize common facets and briefly explain what advanced filters do. **Suggested command:** `$impeccable distill`.
3. **[P2] The score explanation is tucked behind “Score key.”** List appearances are central to the ranking, but users may encounter the count before learning what it means. **Fix:** put a short explanation near the visible count and reserve the disclosure for detail. **Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify`.
4. **[P3] Some catalogue and dialog labels use very small type.** The stylesheet uses 8–11px mono labels in places such as ledger headers and detail metadata. **Fix:** keep the metadata hierarchy while enlarging essential labels. **Suggested command:** `$impeccable typeset`.
