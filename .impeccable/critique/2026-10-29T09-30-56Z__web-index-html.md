## Overall Impression

The catalogue has a clear point of view and strong provenance. Its biggest opportunity is to make the liked-list ranking model immediately clear while letting visitors reach the films before navigating the full set of controls.

## What’s Working

- The almanac visual language and poster-wall/ledger pairing make the page feel built for film discovery and comparison.
- Provenance is inspectable through source lists, appearance counts, the score key, and ranking explanation.
- Recovery is stronger: local preference Undo persists across reloads, and filters have clear active and reset states.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] Clarify that the user liked the lists, not necessarily the films.**
   **Why it matters:** “Films you’ve saved across other people’s lists” can sound like the user saved each film. That obscures the product’s distinguishing signal.
   **Fix:** Describe films recurring across public lists the profile likes; state the list-level action plainly.
   **Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify`

2. **[P2] Reduce competition in the catalogue control row.**
   **Why it matters:** Search, three watched tabs, Filters, Catalogue tools, and Sort present seven affordances before the visitor reaches results. The new filter groups help, but the entry row still asks for several choices at once.
   **Fix:** Keep search and watched status prominent; consider moving a lower-frequency action such as Sort into an existing disclosure.
   **Suggested command:** `$impeccable distill`

3. **[P2] Improve supporting-label legibility and touch comfort.**
   **Why it matters:** The detector flags the 10px demo link and small facet headings; several controls are compact. This can make long browsing sessions and touch use tiring.
   **Fix:** Raise essential control labels and supporting text, and review minimum touch targets at mobile sizes. Treat the container-padding warnings as unconfirmed until rendered; many appear to have child or CSS insets.
   **Suggested command:** `$impeccable adapt`

4. **[P2] Keep edition freshness easy to notice.**
   **Why it matters:** The site uses a manually refreshed snapshot, so its edition date and stale notice help visitors judge how current the catalogue is. Source review can’t confirm their visual prominence.
   **Fix:** Make the stale notice easy to spot and pair it with a concise explanation of what remains usable.
   **Suggested command:** `$impeccable polish`

## Persona Red Flags

- **Alex, power user:** Search shortcut, sorting, saved views, and alternate layouts help repeated exploration. The control row has many peers, and there is no batch action across films.
- **Jordan, first-timer:** The browse action and ranking explanation support discovery. The hero’s “saved across other people’s lists” wording may confuse list likes with saved films; “source lists” and “ledger” may also need a short gloss.
- **Sam, keyboard and screen-reader user:** Skip link, pressed states, live announcements, and Escape handling are useful. The `/` shortcut is visible and declared in `aria-keyshortcuts`, but the search help text does not explain it accessibly.
