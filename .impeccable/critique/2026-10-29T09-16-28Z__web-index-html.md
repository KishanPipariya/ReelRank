## Overall Impression

ReelRank has a recognizable, useful catalogue structure, and search now names its displayed-film scope. The largest usability gap is still the all-at-once Filters panel; the first-open tools group also remains dense despite clearer task grouping.

## What’s Working

- The poster wall and ledger support distinct browsing and comparison tasks within one visual system.
- Ranking provenance is inspectable through list counts, source links, watched progress, edition notes, and the ranking explainer.
- Search now states the displayed-film limit, and preference changes have an Undo path.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] Filters still present unrelated facets together.** Opening Filters exposes genres, source lists, decades, running time, country, and language in one broad panel. This asks first-time visitors to understand many different ways of narrowing films at once. Implement the approved shape: keep Genre and Decade as quick picks, then group the remaining facets under More filters (People & lists, Place & language, Running time). **Suggested command:** `$impeccable polish`.
2. **[P2] Save & revisit is still a dense first-open panel.** It opens with save, choose, apply, delete, import, and export controls visible together. These are related, but the main action is not clearly dominant. Keep Save & revisit first, make saving/applying the obvious path, and tuck import/export into a secondary disclosure. **Suggested command:** `$impeccable distill`.
3. **[P2] Copy does not fully carry the Personal Film Almanac identity.** “Watchlist in orbit” can suggest a manually curated watchlist, while the product ranks films aggregated from liked lists. Align the stamp and hero language with personal editions and list appearances. **Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify`.
4. **[P2] Undo is session-only and nested in Catalogue tools.** It helps with clear/reset mistakes, but closing the tools panel hides recovery, and reloading the page loses it. Keep the recovery action available near the setting that changed, or make the consequence clear before the action. **Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden`.

The emotional valley is the transition into Filters, where a broad set of facet choices appears together. Undo offers reassurance after preference changes, though its availability depends on staying in the page and reopening Catalogue tools.
